2. IMPLEMENTATION

Added services/mira-memory-conflict-assessment.js.

Scope:
- Read-only validation against the v0.2.4 canonical record structure.
- Rejects missing required fields, unsupported top-level fields, invalid enums, malformed timestamps, invalid revisions/hashes, invalid provenance, and invalid access shape.
- Structurally detects the seven contract conflict categories.
- Returns REVIEW_REQUIRED for malformed or unverified records.
- Reports cryptographicVerification=NOT_PERFORMED. It does not claim that contentHash has been recomputed or authenticated.
- Does not write, merge, resolve, quarantine, or mutate records.

Added tests/mira-memory-conflict-assessment.test.js covering:
- Identity, revision, content, source, access, schema and integrity conflict classification.
- Malformed and extra-field fail-closed behavior.
- Unverified record review requirement.
- Read-only/no-write boundary.

5. LIMITATIONS / REMAINING GATES

This is a read-only structural classifier, not the complete conflict lifecycle required by Contract §5. The following remain REVIEW_REQUIRED:
- Durable conflict record containing both original versions, source IDs, timestamps, hashes and conflict reason.
- Quarantine persistence and enforcement that quarantined records are excluded from trusted retrieval.
- Governed resolution, merge/reject actor, resulting revision, and audit correlation.
- Integration at every provider/adaptor write boundary.
- Recompute/verify canonical content hashes under an approved canonical serialization profile.
- Concurrency/compare-and-swap and uncertain-write arbitration.
- Independent verification of conflict evidence.

The classifier must not be represented as resolving or quarantining conflicts.

DR-08 U03/U04/U05/U06 and E-15 remain unresolved/not approved as specified in the Decision Closure Matrix. HFM-004 Drive cutover and HFM-005 encrypted emergency backup remain deferred. The Blueprint source-hash discrepancy remains a separate source-integrity gate until reconciled against an authoritative baseline.

No production Memory/Knowledge was touched. No provider cutover, live API, production migration, deployment, or merge was performed. Version remains v0.2.
